# Design

The caller supplies local evidence bindings with project, field, value, and revision. Confirmed typed facts without exact bindings fail closed. Only the caller can authenticate the source and establish revision freshness. Existing AT-246 and AT-261 functions produce the draft and specialist questions. Wi-Fi is an explicit deterministic camera medium, not an inferred network design.

Validated client assets, evidence-backed absence, and purchase flags in AT-246 are unsupported assertions outside Fact and fail closed in this preview. Typed routing directives are supplied by the trusted caller and carry no compatibility proof.
